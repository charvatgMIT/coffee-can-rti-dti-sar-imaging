Coffee Can RTI DTI SAR Imaging
==============================

Install: drag "Coffee Can RTI DTI SAR Imaging" onto the Applications folder.
Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, on an Apple Silicon or Intel Mac.

First launch
------------
This copy is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so the first time you open it
macOS says it can't verify the app. To allow it once:

  1. Double-click the app in Applications. When macOS refuses, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to "Coffee Can RTI DTI SAR Imaging". Confirm with your password.
  3. When the app asks for microphone access, click Allow: it reads the radar's sync
     and IF signals through the USB audio interface (e.g. the Behringer UCA222).

After that it opens normally.

(Alternative, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Coffee Can RTI DTI SAR Imaging.app")

Using it
--------
Plug in the audio interface (sync and IF on its two inputs) and press Start for live
radar. SAR imaging opens .wav recordings (File > Open, or the Open button on that tab).
The "How it works" tabs and the README explain the processing:
https://github.com/charvatgMIT/coffee-can-rti-dti-sar-imaging
